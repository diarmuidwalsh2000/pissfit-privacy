Privacy Policy for PissFit

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

PissFit ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed to track running fitness and calculate net calories burned. Your privacy is paramount, and PissFit operates strictly on-device with zero remote data collection.

2. Apple HealthKit Data

PissFit requests read-only permission from Apple Health (HealthKit) for running workouts, distance, and active energy expenditure.

  • Strictly On-Device: All HealthKit calculations are computed entirely on your iPhone.
  • No Cloud Storage or Transmission: We do not store, transmit, back up, sell, or share your HealthKit data with external servers or third-party analytics platforms.
  • No Advertising: In compliance with Apple HealthKit Guidelines, your health data is never used for marketing or advertising purposes.

3. Accounts & Personal Data

PissFit does not require an account, profile, email address, or login. We do not track location coordinates or collect personal identifiers.
